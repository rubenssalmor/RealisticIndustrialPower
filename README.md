## Realistic Industrial Power Consumption

Industrial areas are among the highest consumers of electricity.
This mod introduces realistic, size-based power consumption for industrial buildings, ensuring that larger facilities place significantly higher demands on the electrical grid.

Power usage scales with building lot size, encouraging the use of substations and high-capacity power infrastructure. This integrates naturally with the growing ecosystem of substation and power line assets available on Paradox Mods.

By making electricity demand more accurate, this mod adds meaningful strategic depth to industrial planning and power management.

## Features
- Lot size-based power consumption for industrial buildings
- Balanced power values from 0.75 MW (small) to 7.5 MW (very large)
- Configurable global power multiplier (75%–375%)
- Accurate electrical grid demand and consumption behavior
- Compatible with all industrial building types
- Optimized for minimal performance impact

## Power Consumption at 100%
- Small buildings (≤30 tiles): 200–750 kW
- Medium buildings (31–100 tiles): 400–2000 kW
- Large buildings (101–250 tiles): 800–4000 kW
- Very large buildings (>250 tiles): 1900–7500 kW

## Settings
Available in Options → Mod Settings

- Default: 100% (balanced gameplay)
- Range: 75% (lower challenge) to 375% (high challenge)
